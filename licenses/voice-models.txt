VOICE MODELS

The voice models are not bundled with SLC Local TTS. It downloads each one on
demand, the first time a voice is used or when you run its download command,
from the k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx "tts-models" release
(https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/tts-models). Each voice is

Notes

OpenRAIL-M (the Supertonic voices): the licence lets you use the model freely
but forbids some uses, including harmful, deceptive or unlawful ones, such as
impersonating a real person without consent. Anyone you pass the model on to
is bound by the same restrictions. The full licence:
https://huggingface.co/Supertone/supertonic-3/blob/main/LICENSE

Non-commercial voices (CC-BY-NC-4.0 and CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0): these voices may be
used only for non-commercial purposes.

Jenny (en_GB-jenny_dioco-medium): the Jenny TTS dataset's licence asks that
voice interfaces credit the voice as "Jenny (Dioco)". This voice is
Jenny (Dioco).
