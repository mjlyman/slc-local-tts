The voice models are not bundled with SLC Local TTS. It downloads each one on
demand, the first time a voice is used or when you run its download command,
from the k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx "tts-models" release
(https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/tts-models). Each voice is

Notes

OpenRAIL-M (the Supertonic voices): the licence lets you use the model freely
but forbids some uses, including harmful, deceptive or unlawful ones, such as
impersonating a real person without consent. Anyone you pass the model on to
is bound by the same restrictions. The full licence:
https://huggingface.co/Supertone/supertonic-3/blob/main/LICENSE

Non-commercial voices (CC-BY-NC-4.0 and CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0): these voices may be
used only for non-commercial purposes. The two voices marked "Fine-tuned from
Piper's non-commercial Ryan voice" may carry the same restriction, whatever
their own licence says, because Ryan is CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.

Lessac (the voices marked "Fine-tuned from Piper's research-only Lessac
voice"): these Piper voices were made by fine-tuning Piper's en_US-lessac
voice. That voice was trained on the Lessac dataset from the Blizzard
Challenge 2013 (Lessac Technologies), whose licence limits its use to
research purposes. The licence listed for each of these voices covers only
its own training recordings. Whether the Lessac restriction also applies to a
voice fine-tuned from it is an open question. If it matters to you, choose a
voice without this mark. The dataset and its licence:
https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/

Jenny (en_GB-jenny_dioco-medium): the Jenny TTS dataset's licence asks that
voice interfaces credit the voice as "Jenny (Dioco)". This voice is
Jenny (Dioco).
